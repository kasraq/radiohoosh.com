About

Radio Hoosh is a podcast about artificial intelligence and technology, in English and in Farsi.

Every day we pick the AI and tech topics that matter most, from the news, research papers and the developer community. Each one becomes a conversation made with Google NotebookLM.

The episodes are AI-generated: the voices and the conversation are produced by AI from the sources linked with each episode. Check those sources before you rely on a detail.
