About

Radio Hoosh ("hoosh" means intelligence in Farsi) is a podcast about artificial intelligence and technology, in English and in Farsi.

Every day I pick the AI and tech topics that matter most, from the news, research papers and the developer community. Each one becomes a conversation made with Google NotebookLM.

The episodes are AI-generated: the voices and the conversation are produced by AI from the sources linked with each episode. Check those sources before you rely on a detail.

$ whois radiohoosh

I'm Kasra Qasempour. For a while now, NotebookLM has been one of my main ways to learn something new: I give it papers and articles on a topic and listen to the conversation on a walk or a commute. After a few weeks of doing this by hand, I automated it. AI now finds the sources, makes the episodes, writes the descriptions and publishes them; I choose the topics. It's how I learn, and a small experiment in how far an end-to-end AI pipeline can go today.

You can reach me on LinkedIn or at radiohoosh@gmail.com.
